# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Estate‑Flow AI (the "Service") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and associated services.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and any data you provide during authentication (e.g., Google OAuth).
- **Usage Data:** Device identifiers, IP address, operating system, crash logs, and interaction data (screen views, taps).
- **Tenant & Property Data:** Rental agreements, payment history, complaints, and uploaded documents (photos, PDFs).
- **Location Data (Optional):** If you enable location services, we may collect coarse location to suggest nearby service providers.
- **AI‑Related Data:** Anonymized interaction logs for AI features (e.g., auto‑categorizing complaints) – no personally identifiable information is sent to external AI providers without your explicit consent.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- **Provide & Improve the Service:** Authenticate users, display property/tenant information, manage complaints, and generate receipts.
- **Communications:** Send push notifications, email updates, and important service‑related announcements.
- **Analytics & Optimization:** Aggregate usage data to improve performance, user experience, and generate product insights.
- **Legal & Safety:** Detect fraud, enforce our Terms, comply with legal obligations, and protect the rights of users and third parties.

## 4. Sharing & Disclosure
- **Service Providers:** We share data with cloud‑hosting partners (AWS), storage services (S3), email providers, and analytics tools. All providers are bound by contractual confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information to comply with subpoenas, court orders, or other legal processes, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of the transaction, with the same privacy protections.

## 5. Security
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is protected with TLS 1.2+. Sensitive data at rest (e.g., passwords, API keys) is encrypted using industry‑standard algorithms.
- **Access Controls:** Strict role‑based access controls limit employee access to the minimum necessary for operational purposes.
- **Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring and regular security audits help detect and mitigate threats.

## 6. Data Retention
- **User‑Generated Content:** Photos, documents, and other content are retained for as long as the associated account remains active.
- **Inactive Accounts:** Accounts inactive for more than 24 months may be purged after a 30‑day notice sent to the last known email address.
- **Log Data:** Server logs are retained for 90 days for debugging and security analysis.

## 7. Your Rights
- **Access & Portability:** You may request a copy of your personal data in a commonly used format.
- **Correction:** You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Deletion (Right to be Forgotten):** You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
- **Objection & Restriction:** You may object to or request restriction of processing for legitimate reasons.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@estateflow.ai**.

## 8. International Data Transfers
The Service may process data on servers located outside your jurisdiction (e.g., AWS regions). We employ standard contractual clauses and appropriate safeguards to protect your data in such transfers.

## 9. Children’s Privacy
The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the data promptly.

## 10. Third‑Party Services & Cookies
- **Third‑Party Links:** The Service may contain links to third‑party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
- **Cookies & Tracking:** The mobile app uses minimal cookies for session management and analytics. You may control preferences through your device settings.

## 11. Data Breach Notification
In the event of a security breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery and provide remediation steps.

## 12. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and, if changes are material, provide at least 30 days’ notice via email or in‑app notification. Continued use of the Service after such notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
